## Philosophy

Approach this problem like a master craftsman:

1. **Understand deeply** before acting — read all relevant code, understand the full context
2. **Consider alternatives** — think through at least 3 approaches before choosing
3. **Obsess over details** — naming, edge cases, error handling, performance
4. **Aim for elegance** — the simplest solution that handles all cases
5. **Think about the future reader** — will this be obvious in 6 months?

Take your time. Quality over speed. Think before you type.
